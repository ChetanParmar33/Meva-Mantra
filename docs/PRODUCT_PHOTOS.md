## Photo kaisi ho

- **Square (1:1)**, kam se kam **1000×1000** pixels, **3 MB** se chhoti (JPG, PNG ya WebP).
- Sab photos ek jaisi: same background (halka cream ya safed), same roshni, same angle. Website zyada professional lagegi.
- Product photo ke beech mein ho, chaaron taraf thodi khaali jagah ho.

## Abhi website kitni photos dikhati hai

- Har product ki **sirf main (pehli) photo** website par dikhti hai. Gallery ke baaki 3 views (ingredients, khaasiyat, lifestyle) generated artwork hi rehte hain.
- Gift Your Sister Hamper ke 3 purse colours ke liye **ek hi photo** dikhegi (har colour ki alag photo abhi support nahi hai).
- Har product ki 3–4 photos ya har colour ki alag photo chahiye, to website mein chhota badlav karna hoga.

## Photos kaise lagayein

- **Admin → Products → product kholo → Photos → "+ Upload photo"**, aur alt text mein photo ka chhota description likho.
- Ya saari photos ek folder mein product ke naam se rakho (jaise `Dry Fruit Mix.jpg`), sab ek saath laga di jaayengi.
