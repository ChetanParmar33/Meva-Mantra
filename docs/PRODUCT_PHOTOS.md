## 0. Pehle ye padho (zaroori)

- **Abhi website har product ki sirf Image 1 (Main) dikhati hai.** Baaki images (2, 3, 4…) product page ki gallery mein dikhane ke liye website mein chhota badlav karna hoga. Images ban jaayein to bata dena, gallery update kar di jaayegi.
- **AI image asli product jaisi ho:** jar, label ka rang, andar ka saamaan aur size wahi ho jo customer ko milega. Asli packaging ban jaaye to usi ki photo ko AI tool mein "reference" daal kar dobara banao, ya asli photo khinchwao.
- **Label ka text:** AI aksar spelling bigaad deta hai. Do raaste hain:
  1. Text achha likhne wale tool use karo (ChatGPT image, Google Gemini, Ideogram).
  2. Prompt mein `"..."` wale text ki jagah `plain label without any text` likho, phir Canva mein logo aur naam lagao. Logo website mein hai: `apps/web/src/app/icon.svg`.
- **Har image mein same jar:** ek product ki saari images ek hi chat mein banao. Image 2 se aage prompt ke shuru mein likho: `Same jar and label as the previous image.` Ya Image 1 ko reference ki tarah upload karo.
- **Website ka design reference ki tarah:** website par har product ki ek bani hui artwork hai, jisme label ka sahi rang aur layout hai (jaise http://localhost:3100/products/badam). Uska screenshot AI tool mein upload karke likh sakte ho: `Use this as the packaging design reference, make it photorealistic.`
- Free tools jo kaam aayenge: ChatGPT (image), Google Gemini, Ideogram, Microsoft Designer / Bing Image Creator, Leonardo.

## 1. Har image ki settings

| Cheez | Kya rakhna hai |
|---|---|
| Shape | **Square, 1:1** (har image, lifestyle bhi) |
| Size (pixels) | **2048 × 2048** best · kam se kam **1000 × 1000** |
| Format | **JPG** ya **WebP** (PNG bhi chalega) |
| File size | **3 MB se kam** (badi ho to https://squoosh.app free se WebP mein chhoti karo) |
| Har product ki kitni | **3 se 5** (upar summary table mein exact) |
| Pehli image (Main) | Product beech mein, chaaron taraf thodi khaali jagah (website card mein kinare kat sakte hain) |

AI tool mein size ka option ho to **1:1 / Square** chuno. ChatGPT / Gemini mein prompt ke end mein likh sakte ho: `Square 1:1, 2048x2048.`
- **File ka naam:** `<slug>-<number>.jpg`, jaise `mix-dryfruit-1.jpg`. Slug har product ke heading ke niche likha hai. Isse saari photos ek saath sahi product par lag jaayengi.
- **Background aur roshni:** saari studio images ek jaisi (cream background, baayein se halki dhoop). Ye prompt mein already likha hai.

## 2. Common style (har prompt mein already joda hua hai)

Studio images (Main, Andar kya hai, Sizes, Close-up):
```
Photorealistic premium e-commerce product photo, square 1:1, warm cream background (#FBF7EF) with a soft linen texture, soft natural daylight from the left, gentle realistic shadow, sharp focus, true-to-life food colours, clean and minimal, premium Indian brand look. No people, no watermark, no other brand names.
```
Lifestyle images:
```
Photorealistic lifestyle product photo, square 1:1, warm natural light, shallow depth of field with the product sharp and the background softly blurred, earthy Indian home setting, true-to-life colours. No faces, no watermark, no other brand names.
```
**Negative prompt** (agar tool mein "negative prompt" ya "avoid" ka option ho to ye daalo):
```
misspelled text, gibberish letters, extra logos, watermark, other brand names, plastic-looking or deformed food, blurry or melted jar, extra lids, people's faces, extra fingers, cluttered background, oversaturated colours, cartoon, illustration
```

## 3. Packaging jo har image mein same rahe

- **Jar wale products:** clear jar, chamakdaar gold dhakkan, label har product ke apne rang mein (niche har product ke saath likha hai), patla gold border, upar chhota "MEVA MANTRA", beech mein product ka naam.
- **Dry Fruit Khajur Pak:** chocolate brown box, gold mandala-phool ka logo, gold border, "Khajur Pak" gold mein.
- **Khajur Pak gift boxes:** premium square box apne rang mein (emerald, royal plum, burgundy, peacock teal), gold foil mandala, dhakkan par chhoti khidki.
- **Combos aur hampers:** upar wale jar aur boxes hi saath mein.
